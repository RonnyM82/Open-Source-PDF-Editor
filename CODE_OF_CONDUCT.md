# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and considerate in communication.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported privately
to the maintainers via GitHub (open a private
[security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) as a confidential channel, or
contact a maintainer directly). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the
circumstances.
